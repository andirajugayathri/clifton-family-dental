Gum Disease Treatment in Clifton Beach

If you are suffering from soreness, bleeding or swelling in your gums or persistent bad breath, there is a chance you may have gum disease, also known as periodontal disease. This is an uncomfortable yet common bacterial condition that affects people of all ages but is more common in adults aged 30 years or more.

There are two forms of gum disease:

Gingivitis (Mild)

The mild form of gum disease causes irritation and swelling. It can be cleared up with gingivitis treatment.

Periodontitis (Advanced)

The advanced, severe form of gum disease can lead to tooth loss and damage to the jawbone if left untreated. It cannot be cured.

Seeking early interventions such as Gum Disease Treatment (Periodontal Treatment) at the first signs of gum disease is necessary to prevent the condition from worsening. Gum Disease Treatment is a deep cleaning procedure used by dentists to remove the build-up of hardened plaque or tartar in the gums.
